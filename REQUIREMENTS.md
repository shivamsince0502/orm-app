# Take-Home Assignment: AI-Powered Micro-CRM

Build a simple AI-powered micro-CRM for a small business owner who manages relationships with prospects and customers.

The product should help the user, with minimal manual effort:

- Keep track of their relationships and recent interactions
- Understand who may need attention or follow-up
- Quickly understand the relevant context for a customer or prospect
- Decide what action to take next

We will provide a small sample dataset containing customers, contacts, and interactions.

## What to Build

There is no prescribed solution, architecture, UI, or required set of features. Use your judgment to decide:

- Which problems are most important to solve
- What the user experience should look like
- Where AI adds meaningful value
- What information or actions should be surfaced to the user

We are more interested in your product and engineering decisions than in the number of features you implement.

## Scope

Please limit the exercise to approximately 4–6 hours.

You may:

- Use AI coding tools such as Claude Code or Codex
- Mock or simplify infrastructure and integrations
- Skip lower-priority features
- Make reasonable assumptions where needed

The result should be runnable and demonstrate the core experience you chose to build.
